Vegetarian Eats by City

Hand-picked vegetarian-friendly restaurants across Chinese, Indian, Italian, Thai, and Middle Eastern cuisines for every city we'll visit. Big-city stops have lots of options; smaller towns (Yangshuo, Luoyang, Zhangjiajie) lean local. Always verify on Google Maps / TripAdvisor / HappyCow before going — restaurants in China change quickly.

🌱 Helpful Mandarin: "Wǒ chī sù" (我吃素) = "I'm vegetarian." "Bù yào ròu" (不要肉) = "No meat." "Bù yào yú" (不要鱼) = "No fish."
Tip: in mainland China, "vegetarian" sometimes means no red meat — be specific. Buddhist (素食 sùshí) restaurants are 100% safe.
